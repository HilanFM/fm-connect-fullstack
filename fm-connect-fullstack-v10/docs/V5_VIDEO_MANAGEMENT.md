# FM Connect v5 - Video Management Amendments

## Added in v5

### Institute Owner Videos
- Institute owners can upload a video file or paste a video URL.
- Institute owners can upload/change a thumbnail image.
- Institute owners can link a video to a specific course/degree.
- Linked videos appear inside the selected course/degree detail page.
- Institute videos appear inside the institute detail page.
- Institute owners can edit and delete their own videos.

### Counselor Videos
- Counselors can upload guidance videos or paste video URLs.
- Counselors can upload thumbnails.
- Counselor videos appear on the public Videos page for students.
- Counselors can edit and delete their own videos.

### Admin Videos
- Admin can upload platform videos.
- Admin can assign videos to institutes.
- Admin can assign videos to counselors.
- Admin can link videos to courses/degrees.
- Admin can edit and delete all videos.

### Student/Public Video Viewing
- Students and public visitors can see all published videos from the Videos page.
- Course/degree detail pages show linked videos and related category/platform videos.
- Institute detail pages show institute videos like image/gallery content.

### Contact Us Rename
- The previous Contact Us / Reach Us / Inquiry label is renamed to Contact Us.
- The inquiry/contact form feature remains available.
